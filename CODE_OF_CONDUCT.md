# Community conduct

Contributors must communicate professionally, review technical claims against source material, and keep discussion focused on the project. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, credential disclosure, market manipulation, and deliberate publication of private trading data are prohibited.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when this policy is violated. Security reports and personal information must be handled through private channels.

Project decisions should be documented with reproducible evidence, explicit tradeoffs, and respectful review. Financial performance claims require complete risk and protocol context.
